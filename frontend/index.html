Classification Scope

Email Categories

The system separates email content into four categories commonly found in inbox security and filtering tasks.

HAM

Ham

Legitimate email communication without clear signs of spam, phishing, or commercial promotion.

SPM

Spam

Unwanted or low-value bulk email that may contain irrelevant, repetitive, or misleading content.

PHS

Phishing

Potentially malicious email designed to manipulate users into sharing credentials, financial details, or sensitive information.

PRM

Promotion

Marketing-oriented content such as offers, product announcements, discounts, campaigns, and commercial messages.

Technical Workflow

How the System Works

The project follows a structured text classification pipeline from raw email input to security-oriented output.

01

Email Input

The subject and body are combined into one text sample.

02

Text Cleaning

The text is normalized and prepared before model inference.

03

Tokenization

The cleaned message is converted into numerical sequences.

04

Model Prediction

The trained models classify the email into one of the four categories.

05

Security Output

The interface returns the prediction, confidence, probabilities, and recommendation.
